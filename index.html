About Us

Education without borders

TGF is an international nonprofit education organization helping people develop practical skills in English, computers, programming, and artificial intelligence.

Our Identity

Empowering People. Connecting the World.

Our emblem brings together what TGF stands for: the globe for a worldwide community of learners, the open book for education, and the graduate reaching for a star for the ambition and achievement we help every student realize.

Our Mission

To make education and technology skills accessible to people around the world, helping learners build practical skills for education, employment, and the digital future.

Our Vision

A world where language and technology are bridges — not barriers — to opportunity, so that every learner can participate fully in a global digital society.

Our Values

Accessibility, practical learning, community, integrity, and the responsible use of technology and AI.
